============================================================
             DETERMINISTIC NUTRITION ENGINE
============================================================

This is one of the most important components.

Nutrition must be calculated using trusted nutrition data.

Potential sources:

USDA FoodData Central
IFCT 2017
other verified nutrition datasets

The database layer must provide nutrient values.

For each food:

NutrientValue = quantity × nutrient_per_reference_unit

For a meal:

MealNutrient = SUM(all food nutrient values)

For daily nutrition:

DailyConsumed = SUM(all persisted meals for the day)

Remaining:

Remaining = Target - Consumed

Track at minimum:

Calories
Protein
Carbohydrates
Fat
Fiber

Sodium
Potassium

Do NOT let an LLM calculate these numbers.
